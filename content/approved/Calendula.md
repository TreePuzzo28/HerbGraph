  bestPreparations: >-
    *   **The Ultimate Tissue-Knitting Oil:** This is the gold standard for
    calendula. Pack a clean glass jar tight with *completely bone-dry* whole
    flower heads and submerge them fully in organic olive, jojoba, or almond
    oil. Let it infuse in a warm spot for 4–6 weeks. The oil will turn a deep,
    golden marigold-yellow. Strain and use this oil directly, or melt it with
    beeswax to create a world-class skin salve.

    *   **The Vulnerary Covered Infusion:** Steep 2 tablespoons of dried flowers
    in a cup of boiling water under a tight lid for 15 minutes. This strong tea
    can be cooled and used as a soothing wash for weeping eczema, a sitz bath
    for postpartum recovery, or an exceptional, anti-fungal mouthwash for oral
    thrush.

    *   **The Lymph-Moving Tincture:** To extract both the water-soluble
    compounds and the heavy, sticky resins, macerate fresh or dried flowers in a
    90 to 100-proof alcohol for 4–6 weeks. This tincture acts internally as a
    superb lymphatic stimulant.
  preparationNotes: >-
    > [!info] The "Skin Closer" Caution

    > Calendula is a premier **vulnerary**, meaning it physically accelerates
    local cellular mitosis to knit broken skin back together at a stunning rate.
    Because it is so efficient, **never apply calendula salves or oils to a
    wound that is deep, punctured, or actively infected**. If you close the
    surface skin too quickly, you can physically trap deep-seated bacteria or
    pus beneath the surface, leading to an abscess. Always use it on clean,
    superficial scrapes, burns, or wounds that are already healing from the
    bottom up.
  keyChemistryMechanics: >-
    *   **The Cellular Architect (Carotenoids & Triterpenes):** Calendula
    flowers are packed with **triterpenoids, flavonoids, and orange
    carotenoids** (like lutein). These compounds stimulate
    neovascularization—the formation of new microscopic blood capillaries—and
    accelerate collagen synthesis, physically forcing the body to lay down new
    epithelial tissue over raw wounds.

    *   **The Lymphatic Pump:** Taken internally, its active resins stimulate
    systemic lymphatic drainage, physically thinning out congested, boggy white
    blood cell fluids and moving metabolic debris out of swollen, stagnant lymph
    nodes.
  safetyContraindications: >-
    > [!warning] ⚠️ Safety & Contraindications

    > * **Asteraceae Allergy:** Because calendula is a member of the Asteraceae
    family, individuals with known anaphylactic or severe contact allergies to
    ragweed, marigolds, daisies, or chrysanthemums should patch-test a small
    area of skin before using it extensively.

    > * **Pregnancy:** Avoid consuming large, concentrated *internal* medicinal
    doses (like tinctures or heavy teas) during pregnancy due to its traditional
    classification as a mild **emmenagogue** that can stimulate pelvic blood
    flow. Topical application of creams, oils, and salves remains completely
    safe and highly recommended throughout pregnancy.
