  preparationNotes: >-
    > [!info] The Leaf vs. Seed Paradox

    > Cilantro leaves and Coriander seeds come from the exact same plant, yet
    they display completely opposite energetic qualities in herbalism:

    > * **Cilantro Leaves:** Are physically **cooling, fresh, and clearing**.
    They are used to soothe heat in the digestive tract, ease internal
    inflammation, and cool down a hot, over-taxed liver.

    > * **Coriander Seeds:** Are physically **warming, sweet, and stimulating**.
    They act as a classic carminative spice, warming up a cold, sluggish
    stomach, dispelling abdominal gas, and increasing digestive fires.

  safetyContraindications: >-
    > [!warning] ⚠️ Safety & Contraindications

    > * **The Detox Flood Rule:** Because cilantro is a highly capable cellular
    mobilizer of heavy metals, consuming massive therapeutic doses of fresh
    succus or pesto can occasionally overwhelm the body's elimination pathways

    > * **The Genetic Soap Flavor:** Roughly 10% of the population carries a

    to the aldehyde chemicals in cilantro, causing it to taste exactly like soap
    or crushed bugs. If you or your clients possess this gene, substitute
    cilantro with flat-leaf parsley or celery leaf to achieve similar cooling,
    diuretic benefits without the digestive revulsion.
