apothecaryApplications:
  keyChallengesAddressed: >-
    *   Structural weakness: Brittle nails, thinning hair, and bone density
    maintenance.

    *   Tissue trauma: Slow-healing wounds, skin abrasions, and superficial
    bleeding.

    *   Urinary tract tone: Mild fluid retention or boggy, relaxed bladder
    tissues.
  bestPreparations: >-
    *   **Mineral Rich Long Decoction:** Horsetail's main medicine is **silica**
    (plant-based glass), which is entirely insoluble in alcohol and cannot be
    extracted in a standard 10-minute tea. You **must** simmer the dried herb in
    water on the stove covered for a minimum of 20–30 minutes to physically
    dissolve the minerals into the liquid.

    *   **Infused Vinegar:** Apple cider vinegar is a magnificent solvent for
    pulling minerals out of bones and plants. Steep dried horsetail in raw ACV
    for 4 weeks for an incredibly mineral-rich hair rinse or culinary salad
    dressing base.
  preparationNotes: >-
    > [!info] The Alcohol Illusion

    > **Never try to make a standard alcohol tincture of horsetail.** Alcohol
    completely crashes minerals out of solution. If you take a horsetail
    tincture, you are missing out on the exact silica and calcium compounds that
    make the plant famous. Stick to long water decoctions, vinegars, or crushing
    the fresh green plant to express the raw juice.
  keyChemistryMechanics: >-
    *   **The Structural Mechanic:** Horsetail has the highest concentration of
    organic **silica** in the entire plant kingdom. Silica is a foundational
    building block for collagen synthesis, helping the body knit together
    connective tissue, strengthen blood vessel walls, and reinforce the calcium
    matrix in bones and nails.

    *   **The Astringent Mechanic:** High tannin content gives it an immediate,
    physical binding and drying effect on weeping wounds or relaxed, boggy
    mucosal tissues.
  safetyContraindications: >-
    > [!warning] ⚠️ Safety & Contraindications

    > * **Kidney Caution:** Avoid long-term continuous use (more than 4–6 weeks
    at a time). The crystallized silica can mechanically fatigue and irritate
    the delicate filtering tubules of the kidneys if consumed indefinitely
    without a break.

    > * **Thiamin (Vitamin B1) Depletion:** Fresh horsetail contains an enzyme
    called *thiaminase*, which breaks down Vitamin B1 in the body. While drying
    or boiling completely destroys this enzyme (making cooked/dried preparations
    perfectly safe), **never consume massive quantities of the raw, uncooked
    herb** over a prolonged period.

    > * **Cardiac/Kidney Edema:** Do not use horsetail as a diuretic if your
    fluid retention is caused by severe heart or kidney failure.
