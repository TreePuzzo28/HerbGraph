  bestPreparations: >-
    * **The Uterine Tonic Infusion:** Place a generous tablespoon of the dried
    leaves and flowers into a mug, pour boiling water over it, cover tightly to
    trap volatile fractions, and steep for 10 to 15 minutes. This classic tea
    offers a beautifully balanced, mildly bitter, and distinctly astringent
    drink that tones the reproductive matrix.

    * **Hydroethanolic Tincture:** Extracting recently dried aerial parts in a
    mid-proof alcohol solvent (40% to 50% ABV) captures both the water-soluble
    tannins and the plant's unique bitter glycosides. This creates an
    exceptionally convenient, shelf-stable remedy for acute menstrual support.

    * **The Sit-Bath or Topical Wash:** Simmering a large handful of leaves in a
    quart of water for 20 minutes creates a highly concentrated, binding,
    antimicrobial wash. Once strained and cooled to a comfortable temperature,
    it can be used as a targeted sit-bath or external spray to soothe and repair
    delicate perineal tissues postpartum.
  preparationNotes: >-
    * **The Alchemy of the Dew:** The botanical name *Alchemilla* stems from the
    Arabic *Al-kemelych* (alchemy). Ancient alchemists believed that the pure
    dew drops collected from the center of Lady's Mantle leaves possessed unique
    celestial properties capable of assisting in the transmutation of base
    metals into gold, or restoring a youthful vitality to parched tissues.

    * **The Velvet Glove Mechanic:** In traditional Western herbalism, Lady's
    Mantle is regarded as the ultimate "knitter" of torn or relaxed structures.
    It is one of our most elegant astringents because it achieves profound
    tissue tightening and fluid containment without causing harsh, localized
    dryness or irritation. It behaves like a velvet glove—gently wrapping,
    stabilizing, and restoring structural tone to the softest, deepest mucosal
    linings of the body.
  keyChemistryMechanics: >-
    * **Ellagitannins & Gallotannins (6-8%):** Exceptionally rich concentrations
    of condensed and hydrolyzable tannins. These polyphenols cross-link surface
    proteins on contact, causing instant micro-vascular contraction. This seals
    leaking capillaries, tightens boggy uterine tissue, and arrests excessive
    fluid loss.

    * **Flavonoids (Quercetin, Rutin):** Provide potent capillary-stabilizing
    and anti-inflammatory support, protecting pelvic vascular structures from
    inflammatory degradation and structural breakdown.

    * **Salicylic Acid:** Trace amounts within the leaves offer a mild,
    soothing, anti-spasmodic effect that helps take the sharp edge off acute
    pelvic cramping and localized tissue irritation.
  safetyContraindications: >-
    * **Pregnancy Caution:** Because Lady's Mantle exhibits a distinct
    emmenagogue (menses-stimulating) and tonifying downward momentum on the
    uterus, it should **not** be used in large, concentrated therapeutic doses
    during the first and second trimesters of pregnancy. However, it is
    traditionally used with great success under professional guidance in the
    final weeks of pregnancy and postpartum to prime and restore uterine health.

    * **Iron Absorption:** Due to the exceptionally high tannin content,
    long-term internal use can theoretically inhibit the absorption of dietary
    iron and other essential minerals. Take it apart from meals and iron
    supplements.

    * **Baseline Safety:** Outside of early pregnancy restrictions, Lady's
    Mantle is a highly safe, deeply respected, food-grade reproductive tonic
    that works gently and effectively to restore balance where there is leakage
    and loss of structural boundaries.
