  bestPreparations: >-
    *   **Infused Oil & Salves:** Because lavender’s volatile compounds are
    highly lipophilic, they transfer beautifully into stable fats like organic
    olive or jojoba oil. A cold-infused lavender oil or a beeswax-hardened salve
    is an indispensable topical tool for skin regeneration, burns, or rubbing
    onto the temples during a headache.

    *   **High-Proof Tincture:** To capture both its aromatic oils and its
    bitter, digestive compounds, use a fresh or recently dried plant tincture
    using 80 to 100-proof alcohol. A few drops in a small glass of water works
    rapidly to settle sudden panic or acute indigestion.
  preparationNotes: >-
    > [!info] The "Less is More" Dosing Secret

    > Lavender is a paradox: in small, gentle doses, it is an incredibly
    soothing, grounding nervine that sedates a frantic mind. However, **if you
    over-concentrate it**, lavender shifts into a stimulant. A tea made with too
    many buds or steeped for too long can trigger agitation, restlessness, and a
    soapy, perfumed flavor that irritates the stomach. Keep your doses light and
    your steep times to a brief **5–7 minutes** under a tight lid.
  keyChemistryMechanics: >-
    *   **The Neurotransmitter Mechanic:** Lavender's primary volatile
    constituents, **linalool** and **linalyl acetate**, are rapidly absorbed
    through inhalation or ingestion. They physically interact with the central
    nervous system by modulating voltage-gated calcium channels, promoting an
    increase in parasympathetic activity (the "rest and digest" state) and
    easing neuromuscular tension.

    *   **The Tissue Regenerator:** Its rich terpene profile acts as a local
    anesthetic and antiseptic, physically cooling heat in a burn and speeding up
    cellular turnover in damaged skin.
  safetyContraindications: >-
    > [!warning] ⚠️ Safety & Contraindications

    > * **Internal Caution:** Generally recognized as highly safe, but excessive
    internal use of concentrated extracts can cause mild gastrointestinal upset
    or nausea due to the high intensity of the aromatic essential oils.

    > * **Topical Sensitivity:** Always patch-test lavender preparations on a
    small area of skin before widespread use, as some individuals can develop a
    mild contact dermatitis to highly concentrated linalool.
