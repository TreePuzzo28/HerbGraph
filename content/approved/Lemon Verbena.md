    *   Nervous System Hyper-Activation: Chronic anxiety, frazzled nerves,

  bestPreparations: >-
    *   **The Heavenly Calming Infusion:** Lemon Verbena makes arguably the most
    delicious, aromatic citrus tea in the botanical world. Steep 1 tablespoon of
    dried leaves in boiling water for 10–15 minutes **under a tight lid**.
    Drinking this hot tea after a heavy meal instantly relaxes a tight, nervous
    stomach and dispels trapped gas. Taken before bed, it gently prepares the
    central nervous system for deep sleep.

    *   **The Sweet Digestif Glycerite:** Extracting fresh Lemon Verbena leaves
    in vegetable glycerin creates an exceptionally bright, intensely
    citrus-flavored syrup. This glycerite is a magnificent, alcohol-free way to
    settle colic or stomachaches in children and highly sensitive adults.

    *   **Aromatic Culinary Syrup:** Steep fresh leaves into a warm honey or
    simple syrup base. While less strictly medicinal, this serves as an
    excellent, spirit-lifting addition to teas, sparkling waters, or light
    summer desserts to ease situational stress and lift a heavy mood.
  preparationNotes: >-
    > [!info] The Citral Sleep Secret

    > While often thought of simply as a pleasant culinary or digestive herb,
    Lemon Verbena is a highly capable **nervine sedative**. Its intense lemon
    aroma profile is dominated by a volatile compound called **citral**. Citral
    possesses significant muscle-relaxing and mild sedative properties. If you
    find that chamomile isn't quite enough to shut off a racing mind at night,
    blending Lemon Verbena with a touch of Linden flower creates a powerhouse
    evening blend for deep, restful sleep.
  keyChemistryMechanics: >-
    *   **The Neuro-Relaxant (Citral & Limonene):** The rich payload of volatile
    monoterpenes—specifically **citral, verbenone, and limonene**—acts directly
    on the olfactory and central nervous systems, decreasing physiological
    indicators of stress and inducing a physical state of somatic relaxation.

    *   **The Antispasmodic Mechanic:** Active flavonoids in the leaves
    physically relax the smooth muscle tissues of the stomach and intestinal
    tract. This action checks hyper-motility, eases painful digestive cramping,
    and allows the stomach to process food efficiently.
  safetyContraindications: >-
    > [!warning] ⚠️ Safety & Contraindications

    > * **Kidney Caution:** Avoid consuming massive, therapeutic doses of Lemon
    Verbena continuously over many months if you have a history of chronic
    kidney disease, as prolonged heavy exposure to its highly concentrated
    essential oils can occasionally irritate renal tissue. Normal daily tea
    usage is perfectly safe.

    > * **Topical Photosensitivity:** If using the fresh plant juice or a
    topical preparation on the skin, avoid immediate exposure to intense, direct
    sunlight, as the concentrated volatile terpene profile can occasionally
    trigger mild phototoxicity in highly sensitive skin types.
