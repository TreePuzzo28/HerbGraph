apothecaryApplications:
  keyChallengesAddressed: >-
    *   Flatulent Dyspepsia: Gas, bloating, and sluggish digestion after heavy
    meals.

    *   Mild Digestive Spasms: Cramping and griping in the gut with nervous
    tension.

    *   Nervous Nausea: Queasiness driven by stress, travel, or an overworked
    digestion.

    *   Hirsutism / Elevated Androgens: Traditional use supporting healthy
    androgen balance in women.

    *   Mild Fevers: A gentle cooling diaphoretic that encourages healthy
    perspiration.
  bestPreparations: |-
    *   Warm Infusion: A pleasant after-dinner digestive tea.
    *   Tincture: Concentrated carminative and antispasmodic support.
    *   Hydrosol: Cooling, gently aromatic mist.
    *   Essential Oil: Diluted for topical digestive massage.

  safetyContraindications: >-
    * **See [[Spearmint]]:** Mojito mint is functionally identical to spearmint
    and shares its full safety profile. See [[Spearmint#⚠️ Safety &
    Contraindications]] for the baseline cautions.

    * **Essential Oil & Infants:** Never ingest mojito mint essential oil, and
    never apply it undiluted to the face or nose of infants or young children.
    Concentrated menthol near the face can trigger laryngeal spasm and breathing
    difficulty.

    * **Acid Reflux & GERD:** Like other mint-family herbs, menthol relaxes the
    lower esophageal sphincter. This can actually worsen heartburn and acid
    reflux rather than relieve it, so use with caution if you are prone to GERD.

    * **Iron Absorption:** The tannin and polyphenol content can mildly inhibit
    non-heme iron uptake when taken with meals. Space doses away from iron-rich
    foods if deficiency is a concern.

    * **Pregnancy:** Culinary amounts are entirely safe, but avoid concentrated
    therapeutic doses such as tinctures or medicinal tea, given mojito mint's
    traditional use as a mild emmenagogue.

    * **Lamiaceae Hypersensitivity:** Individuals with known sensitivities to
    the mint family (Lamiaceae) should monitor for mild localized skin or
    mucosal reactions.
