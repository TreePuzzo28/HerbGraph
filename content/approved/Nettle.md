  bestPreparations: >-
    *   **The Mineral-Rich Overnight Infusion:** Nettle is the absolute queen of
    nourishing herbal infusions. To pull its massive payload of minerals out of
    the plant structure, place 1 ounce of dried nettle leaf into a quart-sized
    glass jar, fill it to the brim with boiling water, screw the lid on tight,
    and **let it steep on the counter for 6 to 8 hours (or overnight)**. Strain
    in the morning; the liquid will be a deep, opaque, almost black-green fluid
    that tastes deeply earthy and mineral-rich.

    *   **Fresh Herb Succus:** Processing fresh spring nettles through a juicer
    and preserving the vibrant green liquid with high-proof alcohol yields an
    incredibly potent, live-enzyme tonic. A spoonful daily acts as a rapid,
    blood-building remedy.

    *   **Nutritive Herbal Vinegar:** Infusing dried nettle into raw apple cider
    vinegar for 4 weeks extracts its dense calcium and magnesium content
    beautifully. Use it as a base for mineral-dense culinary dressings or dilute
    it for an exceptional hair-strengthening rinse


    Because nettle is so chemically diverse, the different extraction methods
    (alcohol, glycerin, or water) pull out completely different medicines.
