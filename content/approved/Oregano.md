    *   Microbial Overgrowth: Acute respiratory bugs, lingering sore throats,

  bestPreparations: >-
    *   **The Infection-Fighting Tincture:** To fully extract oregano’s
    powerful, resinous antimicrobials, use a high-proof alcohol (90 to
    100-proof). A short-term course of this tincture in warm water acts as a
    potent internal shield against stubborn digestive or respiratory pathogens.

    *   **Antimicrobial Head Steam:** For stubborn sinus pressure or deep lung
    congestion, drop a handful of dried oregano into a bowl of boiling water.
    Tent a towel over your head and breathe the steam deeply to deliver the
    airborne phenols straight to the respiratory linings.

    *   **Infused Vinegar:** Infusing dried oregano into raw apple cider vinegar
    for 4 weeks yields a deeply clarifying, shelf-stable extraction that can be
    used topically as a purifying skin wash or internally as a sharp digestive
    tonic.
  preparationNotes: >-
    > [!info] The Pungent Carvacrol Lid Rule

    > Oregano’s remarkable antibiotic properties come primarily from
    **carvacrol** and **thymol**. These heavy, aromatic phenols volatilize
    instantly when exposed to heat. Whenever you are brewing an oregano infusion
    or tea, you **must keep a tight lid on the vessel** for the entire 10–15
    minute steep time. If you leave the cup uncovered, the very compounds
    responsible for killing pathogens will escape into the room on the steam.
  keyChemistryMechanics: >-
    *   **The Broad-Spectrum Antimicrobial:** The dominant monoterpenic phenol,
    **carvacrol**, physically penetrates and disrupts the outer cell membranes
    of harmful bacteria, viruses, and fungi, effectively neutralizing them
    without the risks of standard antibiotic resistance.

    *   **The Carminative Mechanic:** Its intense, warming volatile oils
    stimulate the secretion of bile and gastric juices, physically warming up a
    cold, stagnant digestive tract and dispersing painful trapped gas pockets.
  safetyContraindications: >-
    > [!warning] ⚠️ Safety & Contraindications

    > * **Pregnancy & Lactation:** Do not use concentrated, therapeutic doses
    (like large amounts of tincture) during pregnancy due to oregano’s strong
    **emmenagogue** properties, which can stimulate uterine blood flow. Culinary
    use is perfectly safe.

    > * **The Gut Flora Rule:** Because whole oregano extracts are so intensely
    antimicrobial, avoid taking high-dose medicinal preparations internally for
    more than 10–14 days consecutively to protect your beneficial gut
    microbiome.

    > * **Essential Oil Warning:** Commercial "Oil of Oregano" (the
    steam-distilled essential oil) is highly caustic and can severely burn
    internal mucous membranes. Always rely on whole-plant extracts like
    tinctures or vinegars for home herbalism, or ensure essential oils are
    heavily diluted in a carrier oil.

    > * **Single drop of essential oil** to 8 ounces of water to stop the
    progression of a bug or stomach upset.  If more than one drop is dispensed
    into the water, dilute it with more water.  Very caustic.
