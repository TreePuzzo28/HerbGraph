  bestPreparations: >-
    *   **Aromatic Hair & Scalp Rinse:** Rosemary is highly celebrated for
    promoting circulation to hair follicles. Simmer fresh or dried rosemary in
    water under a tight lid for 15 minutes, let it cool completely, and use it
    as a stimulating, final hair rinse after showering to wake up the scalp and
    combat thinning.

    *   **Infused Memory Vinegar:** Steeping dried rosemary needles in raw apple
    cider vinegar for 4 weeks yields a highly sharp, mineral-and-oil-dense
    remedy. Use it diluted as a cognitive-reviving morning tonic, or incorporate
    it into culinary preparations.

    *   **High-Proof Alcohol Tincture:** To extract the powerful antioxidant
    resins and volatile oils, infuse dried rosemary in a 90 to 100-proof alcohol
    for 4–6 weeks. A few drops instantly sharpens mental clarity during a
    mid-afternoon slump.
  preparationNotes: >-
    > [!info] The "Rosemary for Remembrance" Diffusion

    > Rosemary’s reputation as a memory enhancer is physically tied to its
    volatile aroma. When studying or performing complex tasks, diffuse rosemary
    essential oil or keep a warm cup of covered rosemary infusion on your desk.
    Inhaling the aromatic molecules allows them to cross the blood-brain barrier
    directly through the olfactory pathway, stimulating mental alertness almost
    instantly.
  keyChemistryMechanics: >-
    *   **The Cognitive Mechanic:** Rosemary contains a high concentration of
    **1,8-cineole**, a volatile terpene that has been clinically shown to
    inhibit *acetylcholinesterase* (the enzyme that breaks down acetylcholine).
    By preserving this crucial neurotransmitter, rosemary physically enhances
    cognitive speed, processing, and memory retention.

    *   **The Vascular Stimulant:** The presence of **rosmarinic acid** and
    camphor creates a mild local irritation that safely dilates peripheral blood
    vessels, physically forcing warm blood upward into the brain and outward to
    cold extremities.
  safetyContraindications: >-
    > [!warning] ⚠️ Safety & Contraindications

    > * **Pregnancy & Lactation:** Avoid concentrated therapeutic doses
    (tinctures or essential oils) due to its mild **emmenagogue** action, which
    can stimulate uterine blood flow and contractive activity. Standard culinary
    usage is perfectly safe.

    > * **Hypertension Caution:** Because rosemary acts as a powerful
    circulatory stimulant and minor vascular tonic, individuals with severe,
    unmanaged high blood pressure should avoid large, daily medicinal doses.

    > * **Iron Absorption:** Rosemary is incredibly rich in polyphenols and
    tannins, which can bind to non-heme iron in the digestive tract. If you
    suffer from iron-deficiency anemia, avoid drinking heavy rosemary infusions
    directly alongside your meals.
