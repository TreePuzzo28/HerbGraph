  preparationNotes: >-
    > [!info] The "Sunlight Medicine" Rule

    > St. John’s Wort is deeply tied to solar energy. It blooms at the peak of

  keyChemistryMechanics: >-
    *   **The Nerve Soother (Hypericin):** The intense red pigment,
    **hypericin**, concentrates heavily in the nerve sheaths when applied
    topically, physical grounding and lowering the firing rate of
    hyper-inflamed, traumatized nerve endings (such as in sciatica or shingles).

    *   **The Neurotransmitter Balance (Hyperforin):** The compound
    **hyperforin** acts as a broad-spectrum reuptake inhibitor. It physically
    keeps more serotonin, dopamine, and norepinephrine active and available in
    the brain's synaptic clefts, lifting heavy moods and restoring central
    nervous system equilibrium.
  safetyContraindications: >-
    > [!warning] ⚠️ Crucial Drug Interactions (The CYP3A4 Pathway)

    > * **The Liver Clearer:** St. John's Wort is a potent inducer of the
    liver's **Cytochrome P450 enzyme system** (specifically the *CYP3A4*
    pathway). This means it physically accelerates the rate at which your liver
    clears pharmaceutical medications out of your bloodstream, rendering them
    completely ineffective.

    > * **Do Not Combine:** Do **not** use internal preparations of St. John's
    Wort if you are taking pharmaceutical antidepressants (SSRIs), hormonal
    birth control, blood thinners (Warfarin), cardiac medications, anti-seizure
    drugs, or immunosuppressants. 

    > * **Topical Exception:** This drug interaction warning applies **only to
    internal usage** (tees/tinctures). The topical usage of the fresh red
    infused oil for nerve pain or muscle rubs does not pass through the liver in
    a way that triggers this systemic clearing pathway, making the oil highly
    safe to use externally.
